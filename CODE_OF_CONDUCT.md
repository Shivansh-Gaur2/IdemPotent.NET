# Code of Conduct

IdemShield is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing ideas.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Provide enough context for others to reproduce and understand a concern.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and coordinated vulnerability disclosure.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `shivanshgaur28@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and may result in content removal, warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
